Coin-Op Cutthroat

Solo with two Squatch Bots, two humans and a bot, or three local players. Protect your own color: blue, yellow or red. All 15 object balls are solid-colored; their numbers don't matter. Pocket any ball, even yours, to keep shooting. Scratch and the third player may steal one of your remaining balls, bringing an eliminated player back into the game. Last player with a ball wins.
