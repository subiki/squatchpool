THE 4T'S • HOUSE RULES

House rules get weird out here.

Pick a weird rule set, learn the twist, and play. No fictional backstory required.

Five playable games. Choose a game to play now. Some edge-case house rules are still provisional; each game explains its active rules.

Playable · Two players

🎱 But Wait, There's More

Clear your group, then your opponent chooses either held ball to return and block your 8-ball finish.

Read the house rules
  1. Remove one solid and one stripe from the back corners of the rack. Start with six of each group plus the 8.
  2. Play 8-ball and clear your group.
  3. When you're ready for the 8, your opponent chooses either remaining held ball—your group or theirs—and places it to interfere.
  4. If the returned ball belongs to your group, clear it before legally pocketing the 8.
  5. The other held ball can be introduced when the other player reaches the 8.

Choosing your own ball can be just as nasty as choosing theirs. The browser game requires a called ball and pocket after the break. Slop stays down but never earns another shot; a wrong-pocket 8 loses. Some fouls remain simplified.

🌲 Caroms (Whacky Pool)

Shoot an object ball into the cue ball first, then carom toward a pocket. Choose your call difficulty.

Read the house rules
  1. Place the cue ball at the head position and break using the 1-ball.
  2. Strike an object ball from your group so it contacts the cue ball first and then caroms toward a pocket.
  3. No rail after contact or failure to contact the cue ball first is a foul. Opponent may leave the cue ball or move it to center.
  4. Beginners play slop; intermediate players call the 8; advanced players call every shot. Different opponents can use different levels.
  5. Clear your group and legally pocket the 8.

Some edge cases are provisional; the playable mode explains its rules.

🦍 Coin-Op Cutthroat

Protect your group of five. A scratch gives another player ball-in-hand and a third player a ball claim.

Read the house rules
  1. Groups are 1–5, 6–10 and 11–15. Choose a group after a legal pocket, as late as the end of that turn.
  2. You may pocket any ball, including your own; keep shooting until you fail to pocket one.
  3. On a scratch, the second player gets ball-in-hand and the third player may claim one of the scratching player's balls.
  4. An eliminated third player can re-enter by claiming that ball. Pocketed balls, even on fouls, stay down.
  5. Last player with a ball remaining wins.

The 8-ball on the break is not an automatic win. Group choice and scratch recovery create the strategy. Some non-scratch fouls are provisional in the playable version.

🎲 Press Your Luck

Clear your group, press for provisional points on opponent balls, and finish on the 8. Bank or kick to double the rack.

Read the house rules
  1. Clear your group before pressing for bonus points by legally pocketing your opponent's balls.
  2. Each opponent ball made while pressing adds one provisional point; you may continue shooting.
  3. Once your group is cleared, you may call the 8 at any time. A legal called 8 earns two points plus your provisional bonus.
  4. Banking or kicking the called 8 doubles that rack's total. A missed legal 8 attempt forfeits provisional bonus and the turn.
  5. Scratch while pressing forfeits the bonus and gives your opponent ball-in-hand. Play continues toward the match target.

These are the current player-approved house rules; see the game for the active interpretation.

🐟 Big Tuna Casserole

Play the two number sequences, call pockets, and navigate money-ball scoring.

Read the house rules
  1. All shots are called. Strike the lowest remaining ball in either independent sequence, 1–10 or 11–15, first.
  2. Clear every stripe (9 and 11–15) before legally pocketing the called 10 to win; lower solids may remain.
  3. The rack puts the 1 at the apex, 10 in the center and 15 in the middle of the back row.
  4. Legally pocketing the 15 earns $1, the 9 earns $2 and the winning 10 earns $3. Money carries between racks.
  5. Fouls give the opponent ball-in-hand and illegally pocketed balls are spotted. The rack winner breaks next.

Based on the creator's 2022 original house rules. Read the active game's money-ball and foul details before playing.
