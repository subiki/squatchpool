How to win

  1. Drag on the table to aim the cue stick, or use arrow keys to fine-tune. Reverse aim lets you drag behind the cue ball.
  2. Adjust power, then tap Shoot. Sink the 1-ball in the glowing top-left corner within three shots.
  3. Practice wins earn 5 points up to five times per UTC day. Points attempts cost 10 points on the first shot: 7 grow the pot, 2 go to the displayed creator tally, and 1 is retired.

Misses use one of your three shots; a scratch or a wrong-pocket make ends the attempt. Win a points attempt to collect the local pot once and close this edition. Practice remains free forever.

About this prototype

Points are saved in this browser only.

No money or monetary value. Points cannot be purchased, transferred, traded, withdrawn, redeemed or spent outside this local arcade demo. No Google login, shared jackpot, public leaderboard, creator publishing or server-side win verification yet. Refreshing during a paid attempt abandons it; the points spent on its first shot are not refunded in this local demo.
