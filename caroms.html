Caroms (Whacky Pool)

Development playtest, not a released mode. Two local players share this device. Strike an object ball into the white ball first, then send a ball to a rail and into a pocket. Provisional playtest choices: a struck ball may score after a legal carom, and an 8 made on the break is re-spotted. Original manuscript: Episode 3. This preview does not count toward sitewide statistics.

Option foul

Incoming player: leave the white ball where it is or place it near table center?

Tap a numbered ball to select the striker and, when a call is required, call that ball. Tap a pocket button, or use the ball and pocket selectors for combination shots. Drag on the felt to aim. Arrow keys fine-tune direction; Space shoots. On a foul the incoming player may move the white ball to center. On a white-ball scratch it returns to center automatically. A player wins by clearing their assigned group and legally pocketing the 8.
