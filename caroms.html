    How to play · rules & controls

    Solo bot or two local players. Caroms is a playable beta. Shoot a numbered object ball into the white cue ball first; after contact, a ball must reach a rail. Pocket balls to clear your group, then legally pocket the 8.

    1. Break with the 1-ball. On each turn, select a numbered object ball to strike, then aim and shoot.
    2. First contact must be with the white ball. After that contact, a ball must touch a rail or it is a foul.
    3. Beginners play slop, intermediate players call the 8, and advanced players call every shot. Each player can choose their own level.
    4. When a call is required, tap the ball and pocket or use the selectors; Undo call clears the choice.
    5. Tap a ball, then drag on the felt to aim. Arrow keys adjust aim; Space shoots. On an option foul, the incoming player chooses whether to leave the white ball or move it to center. A scratched white ball returns to center.

    Playtest interpretations: A struck ball can score after a legal carom; an 8 pocketed on the break is re-spotted. See the original Episode 3 manuscript.

    Optional original story · Episode 3 (tap to expand)

    From J. F. Lynwood's original 2024 Caroms manuscript.

    At The 4T's in the Pacific Northwest rainforest, Big Jim and Giggly Squatch meet over a strange game: they strike numbered balls toward the white cue ball, using it as a backboard. Hudsquatch watches, offering loud commentary.

    Big Jim wins the opening toss and breaks with the 1-ball. Giggly learns that missing the white cue ball is a foul, and that using the white ball to pocket another ball is an illegal shortcut. Across three racks, Big Jim's methodical advanced play meets Giggly's enthusiastic beginner shots. Hudsquatch explains option fouls, center placement, and why their calling rules differ.

    “Ah, Giggly, ya gotta hit the cue ball first!” — Hudsquatch

    Giggly improves as the match unfolds, while Big Jim's patience wins out. Their final handshake leaves J. F. Lynwood looking forward to the next unusual game at The 4T's.
