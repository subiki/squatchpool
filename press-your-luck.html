🎲 Press Your Luck

Each of your own balls earns 1 rack point if you win. After clearing your group, opponent balls earn a pressing bonus at risk. A legal called 8 adds 2; a successful declared bank or kick doubles the rack award.

How Press Your Luck works

Complete rules · scoring, pressing, fouls & examples

Race to a target score across racks. Own balls build your rack award; pressing on opponent balls can grow—or erase—a bonus.

Rack, groups and calls

  1. Start a normal 8-ball rack, position the white behind the head string, and break. An 8 made on the break is spotted and play continues.
  2. The table is open until the first correctly called post-break group ball assigns solids or stripes. After the break, call both the ball and the pocket.
  3. Pocket your own group first. Your own-group balls count one point each toward this rack's potential award, including group balls you made on the break once your group is known.

Pressing your luck

  1. Once your group is cleared, you can shoot the called 8 immediately or keep shooting opponent balls to press your luck.
  2. Each opponent ball legally pocketed while pressing adds one provisional bonus point. You keep shooting when you make a valid called shot.
  3. A miss on the 8 forfeits your provisional pressing bonus and ends your turn; balls already pocketed stay down. A scratch or other foul also forfeits the bonus and gives the opponent ball-in-hand.

Winning and scoring

  1. A legally called 8 wins the rack and adds two points. The rack winner banks their own-group points, any surviving pressing bonus, and the two points for the 8.
  2. Declare a bank or kick on the 8 before shooting. If the cue ball or the 8 actually hits a rail before the 8 enters the called pocket, the entire current-rack award is doubled—not previous banked racks.
  3. Pocketing the 8 early, on a scratch, or illegally loses the rack. The opponent banks whatever own-ball points and bonus they already earned in that rack, but gets no automatic two points for the 8.
  4. The rack winner breaks next. First player to reach or exceed the selected target score wins the match, even if one rack carries them past the target.

Reading the scoreboard

  1. Banked = points locked from earlier completed racks; these cannot be lost by pressing now.
  2. Own balls = one point per ball of your group made in this rack, awarded if you win.
  3. Press (at risk) = opponent-ball bonus that disappears on a missed 8 or foul. If legal 8 = projected match total including the two-point 8, before any successful bank/kick multiplier.

Example: You have 6 banked points, pocketed 4 of your own balls this rack, and pressed for 2 opponent balls. A normal called 8 banks 4 + 2 + 2 = 8 more, taking you to 14. A successful declared bank/kick doubles those 8 rack points to 16, taking you to 22.

Playing on this site: After the break, tap a ball and pocket, aim and press SHOOT (Space also shoots while aiming). Set Bank/kick before your called 8 if you want to risk the double; follow Banked, Own, Press and If legal 8 above the table.
