🎲 Press Your Luck

Two-player local beta. Clear your own solids or stripes, then pocket opponent balls for +1 each. Call the 8 to bank your provisional points plus 2; a declared and successful bank/kick 8 doubles the rack payout. Only the rack winner scores. Rules & provisional interpretations

Call the 8-ball pocket

Select a pocket to attempt the 8. A missed 8 forfeits your unbanked bonus. You may instead shoot opponent balls without calling the 8.

Shot controls · power and spin
Full rules, sources, and provisional decisions

Owner-approved rules: clear your group before pressing opponent balls (+1 each); called 8 earns 2 plus bonus; declared and completed bank/kick 8 doubles rack payout; missed 8 and scratches forfeit that shooter's provisional bonus. Early 8 or 8+scratch forfeits rack; opponent earns only their own provisional points. Race defaults to 15; winner breaks next. Eight on break is respotted.

Playtest interpretations (not original historical rules): First legally pocketed ball after the break assigns solids/stripes; mixed pots assign by the first pocket event. Legal own-group pots retain turn. No called shots required except the 8. First object-ball contact must be own group until cleared, then opponent group or 8. Other fouls give ball in hand. A declared bank/kick only doubles when cue hits a cushion before contacting the 8, or the 8 hits a cushion after contact and before entering the called pocket. Uncalled or wrong-pocket 8 loses the rack. Break scratches grant ball in hand. These choices remain subject to owner approval.
