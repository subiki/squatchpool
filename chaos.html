Squatch's Nightmare enables eight combinable mechanics: a gravitational 8-ball, teleporting pockets, roulette after shots, spawning balls, collision color changes, ball splitting, sabotage bumpers, and escalating stages. This is an experimental toy physics sandbox, not regulation billiards. No accounts, publishing, or competitive scoring yet.
